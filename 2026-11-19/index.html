BEFORE THE OPEN

🗺️ This morning's map

SPX was at 7,324 into the open, 213 points below the est. zero-gamma flip at 7,536. That put the map in negative gamma, with est. net gamma at -94.5bn. The call wall was 7,400, the put wall was 7,300, and the expected move was ±84 points (7,240 to 7,407).

THE SESSION

📈 How it traded

SPX gapped up 66 points to open at 7,389, close to the call wall before the first trade. It reached a high of 7,416, above the 7,400 call wall and the 7,407 upper band. It also traded down through the 7,300 put wall and the 7,240 lower band to a low of 7,174, then closed at 7,176, near the low. The 242-point range was well beyond the 84-point expected move, so the negative gamma call was right. Neither wall held.
